# Canon Guardrails

Rules every contributor — human or AI — must uphold. Changing any of them requires
explicit approval from the project owner.

## Permanent principles
1. **Launch the civilization first. Let history earn the war.**
2. **Every Realm should be complete enough to be someone's only game.**
3. **Risk does not require war.**
4. **Peaceful players deserve systemic depth.**
5. **Money buys opportunity, not Canonical truth.**
6. **The world is continuous in history, not necessarily continuous in geometry.**
7. **Your person travels further than your equipment.**
8. **The economic loop and the evidence loop must remain separate.**
9. **No Universal Human Rank.**
10. **One person. Many contexts. Many civilizations. One continuous history.**

## Evidence and interpretation
- Raw observation is stored separately from any derived interpretation. Derived data
  must reference the raw evidence it came from and can be recomputed or discarded.
- **Declared identity ≠ observed behavior ≠ verified Recognition.** These are three
  different record kinds and are never merged.
- **Never assign an Orientation from a single interaction.**
- Do not fabricate Canonical interpretations. Until the derivation model is defined
  by the project owner, the system stores raw evidence only: actor, target, time,
  location, context, action type, outcome, provenance, verification status.
- Do not invent scientific-sounding values or metrics without a defined basis.

## Canonical pipeline (reserved structure)
`Interaction → Modalities → Constituencies → Dichotomies → Polarities → Affinity →
Signature → Recognition`

Reserved concepts (structure only, no invented semantics):
Grandmeta, Metastrate, Infostrate, Metathymos, Metametrics, Metastrategy,
Metamethodology Quotient.
- **Metastrate** = an individual's accumulated experiential state.
- **Grandmeta** = collective accumulated historical memory.

### How these are enforced in code
See `docs/CANONICAL.md`. In short: records carry a `layer`; derived records must cite
inputs from the previous stage, use a Canon-approved method and contain no
score/rank/worth/suitability keys; Orientation needs ≥ 2 distinct interactions (hard
floor); Recognition needs a Signature and an authorized, non-self, non-economic
verifier; reserved concepts have no value slot.

## People are not scores
- No universal human score. No Universal Human Rank.
- Never infer job suitability or human worth from Orientation or any Canonical data.
- Owning things (Familiars, property, wealth) never defines Canonical Recognition.

## Money and history
Money may buy: access, property, scale, beauty, logistics, infrastructure, opportunity.
Money may **not** buy: Canonical truth, Signature, Recognition, fabricated evidence,
rewritten history. Chronicle entries are append-only; corrections are new entries that
reference the original.

## Content and IP
Use original names, characters and placeholder assets only. Do not copy copyrighted
characters, franchise names or exact assets.

## First-release scope limits
Not in the first release: large-scale (100–300 player) warfare, sieges, massive armies,
advanced combat, cross-planet travel, full work/education products, real-money
marketplace, blockchain, NFTs, full procedural universe, thousands of NPCs,
photorealism, cloud GPU streaming, console deployment.
